These terms help both sides know what to expect before work begins. A project quotation, invoice, written approval or payment confirms acceptance of the terms that apply to that project.

1. Booking and payment

Booking payment of 60% of the total quoted amount shall be made prior to the commencement of any work or service delivery.

The remaining balance is due upon completion, before final files, credentials, unwatermarked media or launch handover are released, unless the quotation states another schedule.

2. Scope and additional work

Work is delivered according to the approved quotation, brief and agreed deliverables. Any additional work or out-of-scope requests not covered in the quotation shall be subject to separate charges and agreed upon in writing before execution.

3. Client cooperation

The client is responsible for providing accurate content, approvals, access, brand materials and feedback within the agreed timeline. Delays in receiving these items may change the delivery date and may create additional costs.

4. Revisions and approvals

The quotation includes the revision rounds stated in it. Feedback should be consolidated and provided by the authorised client contact. Further revisions, direction changes or new concepts may be quoted separately.

Once a design, edit, page or deliverable is approved in writing, later changes may be treated as additional work.

5. Photography and videography

Dates are reserved once the booking payment is received. Rescheduling is subject to availability. The client should communicate cancellations or changes as early as possible. Costs already incurred for locations, transport, crew, equipment or third parties may remain payable.

Onchari Group will make reasonable professional efforts to deliver the agreed coverage, but cannot guarantee specific people, weather, lighting, venue conditions or moments outside its control.

6. Copyright and usage rights

Unless a written agreement says otherwise, Onchari Group and its photographers, videographers, designers and other creators retain copyright and intellectual property rights in the photographs, videos, designs, code, concepts, edits and other creative work we produce.

After full payment, the client receives the usage licence described in the quotation for its own business, marketing and communication purposes. A full copyright transfer, exclusive licence, resale, sublicensing or major alteration requires a separate written agreement and may carry an additional fee.

Raw files, project files, unused footage, working files and rejected concepts are not included unless expressly stated in writing.

7. Portfolio and promotional use

Unless the client requests confidentiality in writing before publication, the client grants Onchari Group permission to display finished work, selected images, short clips, screenshots and the client name or logo in our portfolio, website, social platforms, proposals and promotional materials. We will not knowingly publish confidential information supplied as private.

8. Third-party materials and platforms

The client confirms it has permission to use materials it supplies. The client is responsible for third-party subscriptions, domain names, hosting, stock licences, music licences, platform policies and ongoing service fees unless included in the quotation.

9. Confidentiality and privacy

Each party will take reasonable care of confidential information received from the other. Onchari Group collects contact and project information only to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, deliver work, provide support and maintain business records. We do not sell client information.

Visitors may contact us to ask what personal information we hold, request a correction, or ask about deletion where legally and operationally possible. We may retain necessary invoices, project records and communications for legitimate business, tax and legal purposes.

10. Delivery, hosting and support

Delivery dates are estimates unless a fixed date is written into the quotation. Website hosting, domains, maintenance, advertising spend and ongoing support are separate unless expressly included. After handover, the client is responsible for keeping third-party accounts and supplied content secure.

11. Cancellation and suspension

If a project is cancelled after work begins, the booking payment covers reserved time, planning and work completed to that point. Any unpaid work or approved third-party costs remain payable. Onchari Group may pause work when invoices are overdue or required client materials are missing.

12. Acceptance

By approving a quotation, paying a booking payment, instructing us to begin, or continuing with a project after receiving these terms, the client confirms that it has read and accepted the terms applicable to the work.
